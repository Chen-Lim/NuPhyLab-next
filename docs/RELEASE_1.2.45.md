# NuPhy Lab 1.2.45

## 中文

- 实验室新增拖拽转换：图片、PDF、音视频转换，ZIP / TAR.GZ 打包，以及 ZIP、7Z、RAR、TAR、GZ、BZ2、XZ 批量解压。
- 新增 WebP 输出，保留透明背景；最高画质使用无损压缩。
- 拖拽菜单使用黑灰底色和浅灰选中扇形，配合更清晰的描边与文字；中英文标签适配各类菜单。
- 默认按住 Shift 拖动文件，也可在页面选择一个自定义按键。设置自动保存，并检查输入监控权限和 AI / 框选快捷键占用。
- 所有转换在本机进行，无需 API。保留原文件，输出同名自动编号；进度、取消和最近 20 次记录集中在页面中。

打开 **实验室 → 拖拽转换** 开启。将文件实际放到扇形选项上才会执行；Esc、中心和选项间隙用于取消。自定义普通按键需要输入监控权限。密码压缩包、分卷、动画图片和部分特殊编码暂不支持；WebP 单边最多 16,383 像素。[格式与使用说明](DRAG_CONVERT.md)

## English

- Add Drag Convert in Labs: image, PDF, video and audio conversion, ZIP / TAR.GZ packing, and batch extraction of ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ, BZ2 and XZ archives.
- Add WebP output with transparency; Maximum quality uses lossless compression.
- Use dark gray menu surfaces, a light gray selected sector, clearer outlines and readable Chinese and English labels.
- Hold Shift while dragging by default, or choose a single custom key on the page. Choices save automatically, with Input Monitoring guidance and checks for keys assigned to AI mode or Ask about a selection.
- Processing stays on your Mac and needs no API. Originals are kept and duplicate output names are numbered. Progress, cancellation and the last 20 batches stay on the page.

Enable **Labs → Drag Convert**. Operations start only when files are actually dropped on an option. Escape, the center and gaps cancel. Custom ordinary keys require Input Monitoring permission. Password-protected or split archives, animated images and some codecs are unsupported; WebP supports up to 16,383 pixels per edge. [Formats and usage](DRAG_CONVERT.md)
